# Privacy Policy for OneClick - Full Page Webpage Screenshot

Last updated: August 19, 2026

OneClick - Full Page Webpage Screenshot ("the Extension") values your privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use:
The Extension operates entirely locally within your web browser to capture and save webpage screenshots.
- We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or user information.
- All screen captures and generated PDF/image files are processed locally on your device and are never sent to external servers.

2. Permissions:
- Active Tab / Scripting: Used solely to scroll and render full-page captures of the webpage you are viewing upon your click.
- Downloads / Storage: Used solely to save the generated screenshot or PDF to your local downloads folder.
